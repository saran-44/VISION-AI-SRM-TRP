# Licensing note

Ultralytics, including the library and its distributed weights, is licensed
under AGPL-3.0. Internal use by one institution is different from distributing
the packaged executable to other institutions or customers. Before external
distribution, the owner must choose one of these paths:

1. Publish the applicable source under AGPL-3.0.
2. Obtain an Ultralytics commercial licence.
3. Replace the detector with a detector whose licence permits the intended use.

The provenance and licence of the current model weights and training dataset
have not been verified. Obtain a written answer from legal before distributing
the Windows executable outside the institution.
